## Buyer review choices

Choose one with a dated response:

- [ ] **ACCEPTED** — promised deliverables are present.
- [ ] **MISSING_PROMISED_SECTION** — identify the specific accepted-scope item that is missing.
- [ ] **CLARIFICATION_REQUESTED** — request explanation within the accepted clarification allowance.
- [ ] **SCOPE_EXPANSION_REQUESTED** — new work requiring a separate proposal and agreement.
- [ ] **CANCELLED_OR_DISPUTED** — preserve evidence and route through the human approval process.

Silence counts as acceptance only when the final counsel-reviewed agreement expressly defines that mechanism and the required review window has actually expired.

## Correction versus new work

- A missing promised section may be corrected under the original scope.
- A new repository, implementation task, deployment, settings change, additional round, new deadline, or new deliverable requires change control.
- No automated system may classify a disagreement, issue a refund, admit liability, waive a right, or settle a claim.

## Payment and horizon boundary

Delivery does not itself prove payment, settlement, customer count, revenue, profit, valuation, or achievement of a Fardarter Drive horizon. Money remains governed by private external-provider evidence and the canonical revenue record.
