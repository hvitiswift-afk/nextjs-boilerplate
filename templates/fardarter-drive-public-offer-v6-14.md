## What happens after a request

A qualifying exact-prefix `[Audit request]:` issue may receive bounded fit labels and a nonbinding intake receipt. When the requester explicitly authorizes GitHub contact, the repository-native workflow may post **one** public-safe clarification in that exact issue.

Fit approval, an acknowledgement, a label, a comment, a reaction, a file, message delivery, or silence does not create buyer consent, `HUMAN_ACCEPTED`, an order, a capacity reservation, a contract, an invoice, payment, revenue, work start, delivery acceptance, or a canonical event.

A private owner-only work package may be prepared after fit approval. Private Google Drive URLs, file IDs, identities, consent evidence, signatures, authentication records, provider records, banking information, counsel notes, and private deliberations are not published.
