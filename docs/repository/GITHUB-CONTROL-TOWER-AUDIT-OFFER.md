## Capacity and backpressure

Fardarter Drive v6 has three distinct capacity values:

1. **Total planning capacity:** 1,000 slots.
2. **Standard ACTIVE ceiling:** 100 deliveries.
3. **Effective ACTIVE ceiling:** 100 unless a complete override is canonically active.

At the effective ceiling, new paid work starts pause. Intake, fit review, analysis, scope drafting, agreement drafting, and queue preparation may continue.

A public issue, label, comment, draft, invoice, or private Drive folder does not consume or reserve a commercial slot by itself.

## Above-100 active capacity

**Above 100 is allowed.** It requires a `CAPACITY_OVERRIDE_ACTIVE` receipt that identifies:

- exact new active ceiling from 101 through 1,000;
- scope and effective conditions;
- expiration or review point;
- staffing or automation readiness;
- quality-control and acceptance readiness;
- security, privacy, insurance, legal, accounting, tax, support, and incident readiness;
- backpressure and rollback ceiling;
- JP authorization and immutable evidence.

An approval workflow returns `APPROVED_NOT_ACTIVE`. It does not silently change the effective ceiling. Canonical activation must be reviewed, merged, and independently read back. An override may not exceed total planning capacity and does not prove customers, demand, orders, revenue, valuation, funding, or outcomes.
