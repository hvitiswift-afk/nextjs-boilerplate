## Document status law

Every automated commercial document must carry one of these states:

```text
DRAFT_NOT_SENT
DRAFT_REVIEWED
SENT_NOT_ACCEPTED
ACCEPTED_BY_PARTIES
SUPERSEDED
CANCELLED
```

Only `ACCEPTED_BY_PARTIES` may be treated as a binding commercial document, and only after the final agreement conditions are actually met. A GitHub commit or generated file does not create acceptance.

## Indemnity and liability readiness

No document in this repository is “indemnity-proof.” Enforceability and risk allocation depend on the parties, facts, jurisdiction, transaction, insurance, negotiation, and applicable law.

The readiness package must address, as applicable:

- party identity and signing authority;
- exact scope and excluded actions;
- buyer-provided specifications, data, content, and authority;
- third-party claims and intellectual-property allocations;
- indemnity trigger, scope, exclusions, notice, defense control, cooperation, and settlement approval;
- liability cap and excluded damages;
- warranties, disclaimers, no-reliance, and acceptance;
- confidentiality, privacy, security, and data retention;
- insurance and proof-of-coverage requirements;
- governing law, venue, arbitration or court process, and fee allocation;
- survival, severability, amendment, assignment, and order of precedence;
- exceptions that counsel determines should not be limited, including intentional misconduct or bad faith.

See `docs/repository/INDEMNITY-LIABILITY-READINESS.md`.
