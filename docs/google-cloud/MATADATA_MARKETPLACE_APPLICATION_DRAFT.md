# MATADATA Armitron Digital Human — Google Cloud Marketplace Application Draft

- Primary route: AI agent
- Secondary route: integrated software as a service

## Proposed product name

**MATADATA Armitron Digital Human**

The name remains subject to legal, trademark, product-positioning, and Google
review.

## Draft one-line description

A consent-gated human-agent browser, authentication-state, pressure-intent,
continuity, and encrypted recall system coordinated by the Armitron runtime.

## Draft overview

MATADATA Armitron Digital Human helps a user and an AI agent work through
browser, account, email, GitHub, Calendar, and documentation workflows while
preserving human final authority. The system combines:

- Armitron time, watchdog, deadline, lockout, and receipt controls;
- MATADATA pressure intent and protected-step handoff;
- Mind System, Body System, Digital Self, and Agent Continuity Bridge;
- encrypted backward-looking notes with macro, meso, micro, and nano recall;
- authentication, authorization, consent, provider-readback, expiry, and
  revocation states;
- no-blind-retry and append-only correction receipts.

## Intended customer value

- reduce context loss during long or complex browser workflows;
- improve human review and handoff around protected provider steps;
- preserve a verifiable history without putting credentials in logs;
- separate user report, local observation, inference, and provider confirmation;
- support accessibility and continuity needs through user-controlled context;
- provide consistent timing and receipt semantics across browser, email, source
  control, and remote app tools.

## Human authority and safety statement

The product does not automate passwords, passkeys, MFA, CAPTCHA, liveness,
biometrics, identity attestation, signatures, legal acceptance, payment,
recovery, or final consequential actions. Those remain human-only.

Digital Human is a symbolic, user-controlled software and accessibility layer.
It does not claim mind reading, neural capture, biological body capture,
consciousness transfer, legal identity transfer, automatic consent, hidden
surveillance, independent personhood, or independent legal authority.

## Draft deployment model

```text
Customer / authorized user
        ↓ authenticated web or ChatGPT app session
MATADATA Armitron control plane on Google Cloud
        ↓ scoped provider authorization
Browser, Gmail, GitHub, Calendar, and approved customer systems
        ↓
Encrypted customer-controlled notes + digest-chained receipts
```

This is a proposed architecture, not a currently deployed Google Cloud product.
